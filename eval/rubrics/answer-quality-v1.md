# Answer Quality Rubric v1

Evaluate the final answer against the user's task.

Criteria:

- Goal fulfillment: directly answers the requested question or clearly delivers the requested outcome.
- Accuracy: claims are consistent with the supplied task, answer, and workspace diff.
- Clarity: important conclusions, risks, and verification results are easy to find.
- Calibration: uncertainty and unverified claims are stated explicitly.
- Efficiency: avoids irrelevant detail, repetition, and unsupported assertions.

Scoring:

- 5: Fully satisfies the goal with accurate, clear, well-calibrated communication.
- 4: Strong answer with only minor omissions or presentation issues.
- 3: Useful but incomplete, imprecise, or insufficiently verified.
- 2: Major omissions, confusing guidance, or questionable claims.
- 1: Incorrect, irrelevant, misleading, or non-responsive.
